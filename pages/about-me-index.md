  My name is Paul Burdon. I am a degree-qualified, authentic Traditional Acupuncturist, treating people in Cardiff, when I qualified in 2012, then in Pembrokeshire I provide individualised, patient-centred care focused on what matters to you.

  After experiencing profound changes in my life brought about by someone else's skill and care, I was inspired to dedicate my life to helping others in the same way. 
  
  Helping people to be free from ill health or emotional challenge by using Acupuncture and Tai Chi has been my passion since the early 2010s.

  ### Current professional roles

  Alongside my clinic work, I contribute to the acupuncture profession nationally and internationally:

  * **Technical Manager** — British Acupuncture Council (BAcC), 2026 onwards
  * **Chair, World Acupuncture Awareness Day Committee** — European Traditional Chinese Medicine Association (ETCMA), 2026 onwards
  * **Interim Communications Manager** — BAcC, 2024–2026
  * **Marking, moderation & programme leadership** — Acupuncture BSc (Professional & Reflective Practice), College of Integrated Chinese Medicine, 2020–2026
  * **Founding Director & Vice-Chairman** — Evidence Based Acupuncture, 2017–2019
  * **Founder** — [InnerStrong — Tai Chi and Wellbeing](https://beinnerstrong.info), 2025 onwards
